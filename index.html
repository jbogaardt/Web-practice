What we do

If it's faux rock, we can restore it. Every job is different, so we quote each one after hearing about it or seeing it.

Repair

Cracks, chips, broken edges and failed seams in sculpted concrete, shotcrete and fiberglass rock.

Repainting & recoloring

Faded, stained or peeling rock brought back with layered, natural-looking color.

Sealing & maintenance

Protective sealing and regular upkeep to keep rockwork looking good through Utah sun, freeze and thaw.

Waterfalls & streams

Leaks, water stains, mineral buildup and tired finishes on faux rock water features.

Grottos & pools

Rockwork around pools, ponds and grottos, including touch-ups and full refinishing.

Anything faux rock

Walls, planters, boulders and commercial or themed rock. Not sure? Ask.
