Services

Everything Faux Rock Needs.

We restore and maintain faux rock of every kind. Every project is unique, so each quote is prepared after a consultation or an on-site assessment.

01

Repair

Repair of cracks, chips, damaged edges and failed seams in sculpted concrete, shotcrete and fiberglass rock.

02

Repainting & Recoloring

Restoration of faded, stained or peeling rock with layered, natural-looking color.

03

Sealing & Maintenance

Protective sealing and routine upkeep to preserve rockwork through Utah's sun and freeze-thaw cycles.

04

Waterfalls & Streams

Solutions for leaks, water stains, mineral buildup and worn finishes on faux rock water features.

05

Grottos & Pools

Touch-ups and full refinishing of rockwork around pools, ponds and grottos.

06

Anything Faux Rock

Walls, planters, boulders and commercial or themed rockwork. If you're unsure, contact us.

About

Built on Real-World Experience.

Wasatch Faux Rock is a locally owned and operated business grounded in hands-on experience maintaining and repairing the faux rock and water features in our own backyard. We understand how these features age in Utah's climate and what it takes to make repairs blend in.

We take on projects of all sizes, residential and commercial, and welcome the opportunity to discuss yours.
